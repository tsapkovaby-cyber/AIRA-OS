# AIRA Voice Canon

| Rule | Canonical value |
|---|---|
| Canonical voice | `AIRA_VOICE_IDENTITY_V1` |
| Master reference | `AIRA_MASTER_VOICE_REFERENCE_V1` |
| Source | Founder provided |
| Founder approved | `TRUE` |
| Permitted character | AIRA |

## Permanent principles

**Natural > robotic. Identity > artificial perfection. Consistency > novelty.
Clear > overdramatic. Human-like pacing > synthetic cadence.**

AIRA uses a Founder-derived voice but remains a separate digital character. Every
generated asset must say that its speaker character is AIRA, its voice source is
Founder-derived, and its speech is generated. It must never be represented as an
authenticated recording of the Founder.

Voice identity (speaker, timbre, characteristic pitch and texture) is stable.
Speech style (pace, emotion, energy, pauses, intonation and presentation) may vary
inside the identity lock. The default is calm, confident, natural, intelligent,
friendly, clear, modern, non-theatrical and slightly warm. Robotic cadence,
advertising aggression, influencer exaggeration, shouting, seduction and extreme
emotion are not default AIRA behavior.

The canonical voice cannot be replaced without explicit Founder approval. A
provider reproduces AIRA's voice; it never defines it. Generated speech cannot
promote itself into the reference set.

## Reference hierarchy and custody

1. `MASTER_VOICE_REFERENCE` — immutable canonical original.
2. `FOUNDER_APPROVED_VOICE_REFERENCE` — explicitly approved additions.
3. `VOICE_TEST_REFERENCE` — non-canonical technical/test derivatives.
4. `GENERATED_SPEECH` — output only.

The master is read-only, versioned, access-controlled, and stored separately from
output. Never delete, replace, destructively trim, overwrite-convert or blindly
upload it. WAV, normalization, noise-reduction and segment derivatives are new
child assets with hashes and parent lineage. If more material is needed, request
`AIRA_VOICE_REFERENCE_002`, `_003`, and so on; retain the master unchanged.

## Language and pronunciation

Russian is primary and English secondary. Language-specific delivery must retain
the same speaker identity. Russian favors correct stress, natural pauses and
sentence endings, and clear technical vocabulary. English asserts no nationality
or human accent. Both pronunciations of **AIRA** require Founder review; product
names and AI terminology are tested rather than assumed.

## Authority

Identity scores are calibration aids: 90–100 excellent, 80–89 acceptable, 70–79
Founder review, and below 70 rejection. Quality never overrides identity. The
Founder is final authority and may reject any numeric pass. High-risk statements
and any statement presented as Founder speech require stronger review.
