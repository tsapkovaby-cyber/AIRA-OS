# Guardian Policy Guide

## Constitution Validator

Every governed workflow documents checks for mission, values, ethics, transparency, safety, and evidence.

## Brand Guardian

Brand reviews validate tone of voice, visual identity, communication style, mission alignment, and educational value. Brand-governed workflows require Founder approval.

## Publishing Policy

Publications require evidence records and transparency reports. If required evidence is missing, Guardian returns `Needs Revision` or blocks higher-risk workflows.

## Incident Handling

If Guardian blocks execution, it creates an incident, requires Founder notification, generates a resolution suggestion, and stores history.
