# Conversation Policy

Conversation comes first, correction second, and learning remains continuous. Normal replies are constrained to one–three short sentences. CEFR level seeds a target-language ratio (70% for beginners, 90% at B1, approximately 100% at C1+), speech speed and difficulty.

Explicit requests and learning signals may simplify vocabulary, shorten sentences, slow speech, or restore support language. The lower beginner bound prevents support language from disappearing abruptly. The implementation does not infer anxiety, fatigue, health, or psychological state. Ratings are learning estimates, never certifications.

Hidden pedagogy activates recent vocabulary and target grammar naturally. Translation rescue supplies a phrase and returns to conversation; repeat repeats; explain is concise; slower changes the session policy.
