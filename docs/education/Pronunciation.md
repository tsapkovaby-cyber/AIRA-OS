# Pronunciation

Pronunciation sessions demonstrate a sound or phrase, accept a learner attempt, provide specific feedback, retry, and optionally contrast a minimal pair. `PronunciationTarget` records sound, word, phrase, stress, common error, attempt, score, feedback, retry count, and mastery.

RU → EN may focus on /θ/, /ð/, /w/–/v/, vowels, stress, linking, and reduction. EN → RU may focus on ы, р, consonant softness, unstressed vowels, stress, and clusters. Scores are instructional estimates, not diagnoses or formal assessments.
