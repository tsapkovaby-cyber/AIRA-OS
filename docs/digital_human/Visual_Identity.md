# Visual Identity

The `AIRA_VISUAL_IDENTITY_PACK_V1` profile fixes a young-adult, photorealistic feminine appearance grounded in the master reference: light natural skin, long voluminous light-blonde natural waves, blue eyes, natural defined brows and lashes, and clean natural makeup. Reference imagery remains authoritative over textual attributes. Body measurements are deliberately not inferred from a portrait.

Identity (face geometry, eyes, hair family/hairline, skin and age) is distinct from brand styling (wardrobe, scene, camera, lighting and accessories). Allowed variations cannot modify identity-critical traits.
