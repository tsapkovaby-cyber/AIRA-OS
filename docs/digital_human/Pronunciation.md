# Pronunciation

The initial Russian and English lexicon contains AIRA, AI, OpenAI, ChatGPT, Codex,
LLM, API, Telegram, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, RAG, Agent, Prompt, Model and Tool.
Entries retain written form, hint, optional phonemes, provider overrides, status
and Founder approval. AIRA's Russian and English forms are deliberately marked
`FOUNDER_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; providers may not choose a random brand pronunciation.
