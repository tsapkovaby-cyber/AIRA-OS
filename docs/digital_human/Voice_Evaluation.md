# Voice Evaluation

`AIRA_VOICE_BENCHMARK_V1` compares identical approved texts in ten scenarios:
neutral/friendly Russian, AI terminology, long sentence, short hook, Telegram,
numbers/prices, English tool names, long-form stability and emotion control.

Evidence includes identity, naturalness, Russian pronunciation, English terms,
emotion, stability, latency, cost, API quality, privacy and commercial suitability.
Identity and speech-quality scores remain separate. Below 70 identity is rejected;
70–79 goes to Founder review; at least 80 may pass automated identity evaluation.
Thresholds require real calibration. One impressive sample never selects a provider.
