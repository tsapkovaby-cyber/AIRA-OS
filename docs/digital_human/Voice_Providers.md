# Voice Providers and Routing

Adapters expose synthesis, capabilities, cost estimation, health, reference,
clone, emotion and streaming capability. Streaming is interface readiness only.

Providers move through discovered, researched, risk-reviewed, Founder-approved,
testing, approved and disabled states. Before controlled upload, research records
privacy, retention, provider training, commercial rights, deletion, API, cost and
language support. Routing rejects non-approved providers and prioritizes identity
similarity before language quality, emotion, latency, cost, privacy, long-form
stability and commercial suitability. External voice IDs remain replaceable
provider profiles, never canonical identity.
