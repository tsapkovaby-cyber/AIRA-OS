# Voice Security

Consent is `FOUNDER_PROVIDED`, `APPROVED_FOR_AIRA`, automatic third-party sharing
false, and provider upload `REQUIRES_POLICY_CHECK`. Consent cannot silently expand.
Generation is limited to approved AIRA purposes and approved text. Financial,
legal, medical, political persuasion, identity-sensitive and apparent-Founder
speech requires Founder review. Guardian rejects wording changes, pronunciation or
identity drift, defects, unsafe words and brand mismatch.

Assets preserve generation disclosure and parent/hash lineage. Post-processing
creates children rather than overwriting synthesis. The model is ready to carry
provenance, content-credential and platform-label metadata without assuming an
audible watermark.
