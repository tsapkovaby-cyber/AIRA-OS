# Brand Profile

The initial direction is photorealistic, modern, clean, premium, natural, approachable and minimal. Palette: white, neutral gray, soft black, soft violet and controlled burgundy. The recurring signature is a white relaxed shirt, natural blonde hair, blue eyes, minimal jewelry, a modern workspace and restrained violet accent. Cyberpunk, heavy CGI, fantasy, plastic skin and excessive retouching are negative constraints, not defaults.
