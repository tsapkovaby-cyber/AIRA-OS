# Speech Profiles

Supported task profiles are `aira_voice_telegram`, `aira_voice_short_video`,
`aira_voice_long_video`, `aira_voice_educational`, `aira_voice_intro`, and
`aira_voice_experiment`; `aira_voice_realtime` is reserved for later work.

Telegram is warm, brief, direct, conversational and less scripted. Short form is
slightly faster with a clear hook and shorter pauses. Long-form and educational
speech use relaxed pacing, natural pauses, restrained emphasis and stability.
Controlled emotions are neutral, friendly, curious, explanatory, confident,
subtly excited, serious, thoughtful and soft. `AIRA_DEFAULT` is calm, friendly,
confident and slightly warm. Paces are slow, normal, dynamic, short-form and
long-form; post-synthesis speed-up must not damage naturalness.
