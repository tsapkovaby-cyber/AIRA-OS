# Evidence Policy

Every important factual claim must resolve to a registered source. Research and knowledge IDs preserve the internal path from research item through knowledge node and source to claim. Public rendering may omit internal identifiers but storage must retain them.

First-person testing language is permitted only for a `TEST_RESULT` claim with a recorded experiment ID and source. Otherwise renderers must use attribution such as “Based on available documentation.” Estimates, predictions, opinions, and unverified information must retain their labels. Commercial and affiliate relationships are always disclosed; Guardian owns the final disclaimer decision.
