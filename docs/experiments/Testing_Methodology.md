# Testing Methodology

Comparisons must provide equivalent inputs, intent, assets, settings, evaluation
criteria, versions, tiers, hardware/environment, date, and region where relevant.
Rubrics are declared before scoring. Subjective scores retain evaluator identity and
rubric; model grading is not objective truth. Reports expose sample size, all failure
records, confidence, and limitations. Material benchmark changes require a new
benchmark/version and disclosure rather than cross-version equivalence.

Confidence combines sample-size adequacy, observed success ratio, and linked-evidence
coverage. A sample below three is always `VERY_LOW` and receives an explicit warning.
This conservative calculation is transparent and intentionally replaceable by a
future constitution-approved policy.
